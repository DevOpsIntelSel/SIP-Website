A name and a company go in.
A meeting plan comes out.

SIP runs the same cycle an intelligence analyst runs, adapted for a sales conversation. Here's what happens in the two minutes between the two.

01

Define what you need to know

Who the buyer is, what their company is dealing with right now, and what would make this conversation worth a second one.

02

Collect public signals

Professional profile, posts, company site, news, and other public sources. Separate agents work the company and the person in parallel.

03

Analyze: turn facts into meaning

What matters to this buyer, how they decide, where the risks are. Facts alone don't earn a second meeting; the read does.

04

Check assumptions and rate confidence

Agents review each other's work using structured analytic techniques. Every claim carries a confidence rating so you know what's solid and what's inferred.

05

Deliver one page you can act on

Research, ten ranked strategies, and a live battle card for the meeting itself. Built for the seller, not the analyst.
