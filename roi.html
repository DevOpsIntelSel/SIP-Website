How the math works

Additional second meetings = first meetings × (target conversion minus current conversion). Additional customers = additional second meetings × your close rate. Additional value = additional customers × lifetime value.

The defaults are an illustration, not a benchmark. The 25% starting point reflects what many teams see; the 50% target is where we're aiming, not a promised result. Your numbers are the ones that matter.
